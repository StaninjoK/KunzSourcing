How we work

One process for every category.

Whatever the product, the sequence is the same: requirement, supplier matching, validation against documents, commercial coordination, export coordination from Montevideo.

  1. 01

    Requirement

    You define the product, specification, quantity, destination and timing.

  2. 02

    Supplier matching

    We match the requirement to a mill or plant and confirm capacity, listing and lead time.

  3. 03

    Product validation

    Specifications are checked against certificates and plant documentation before anything is quoted.

  4. 04

    Commercial coordination

    Quotation, contract and payment terms per operation.

  5. 05

    Export coordination

    Documents, loading and freight with licensed Uruguayan partners.
