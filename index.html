Uruguay as a sourcing origin

Why professional buyers look to Uruguay.

A small country with an outsized position in wool: a pastoral farming system, an industrial base that processes fibre at origin, and export institutions that European buyers can verify.

Kunz Sourcing does not farm or process wool itself. We work with established Uruguayan mills and producers and put our name on the coordination: supplier selection, validation, documentation and export.

01

Grown on grass, year-round

Sheep graze natural pasture in a temperate climate. Wool here is a product of extensive farming, not of sheds and feedlots.

02

Processed at origin

Uruguay is one of the world's leading exporters of wool tops. Most of its clip is scoured or combed in-country, so buyers can source semi-processed fibre directly from the origin.

03

Independently tested

IWTO-licensed laboratories in Montevideo test lots for yield, mean fibre diameter, vegetable matter and length. The certificates travel with the goods.

04

Reliable to trade with

An investment-grade economy with stable institutions, a national livestock traceability system and regular container services from Montevideo to Northern Europe and the Mediterranean.

How we work

Five steps between your requirement and a loaded container.

A sourcing partner is only useful if the process is predictable. This is ours, and it is the same for a first container and a yearly programme.

  1. 01

    Requirement

    You define product form, fineness, quantity, destination and timing. We reply with what is realistic from Uruguay — including what is not.

  2. 02

    Supplier matching

    We match the requirement to a producing mill or supplier and confirm capacity and lead time before we quote anything.

  3. 03

    Product validation

    Specifications are checked against lot certificates. Samples and independent IWTO test certificates are arranged on request.

  4. 04

    Commercial coordination

    Quotation, contract and payment terms are agreed operation by operation. Pricing follows the wool market at the time of order.

  5. 05

    Export coordination

    With a licensed customs broker and freight forwarder in Uruguay we coordinate bale marking, documents and loading at the Port of Montevideo.

We do not publish price lists or minimum quantities. Wool is traded on the market of the day, and a serious quotation needs your specification first. Terms are discussed individually with each buyer.

Quality & documentation

Paper you can check before you commit.

No certificate, no offer. These are the documents behind a Kunz Sourcing wool lot, from the mill's own laboratory to the export file.

Mill quality certificate

Issued for every combing lot by the mill's laboratory.

  • Fineness (Airflow)
  • Hausman mean length, barbe and CVH (Almeter)
  • Short-fibre content below 40 mm and 30 mm
  • Sliver evenness (CV %) and residual grease
  • Neps and pinpoints per 100 g
  • Vegetable matter, jute and polypropylene
  • Dark and coloured fibres per 25 g

IWTO test certificates

Issued by LATU, Uruguay's IWTO-licensed test house, on request or as agreed in the contract.

  • Wool base and vegetable matter base
  • Mean fibre diameter (LaserScan) and its CV
  • Commercial yields and clean masses
  • Conditioning certificate and invoice mass
  • Bale-by-bale packing list with gross, tare and net weights

Export file

Prepared with a licensed customs broker in Montevideo.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of lading
  • Certificate of origin as required by the destination
  • Mill certification: Oeko-Tex® Standard 100, on request
  • Samples of the offered lot, on request
