Product forms

Two forms, one origin.

Which form you need depends on your process. Both come from the same partner mill and the same Uruguayan clip.

01 · Wool tops

Combed tops for worsted spinning

Scoured, carded and combed sliver, ready for gilling and worsted spinning. Tops are the most processed form of raw wool: short fibres, neps and vegetable matter have been combed out and the fibre lies aligned in a continuous sliver. This is the product form that accounts for most of Uruguay's wool exports.

02 · Scoured wool

Scoured wool for woollen processing

Greasy wool that has been sorted, scoured and dried, with grease, suint and most of the dirt removed. Supplied for woollen spinning, carding, felting and nonwovens, or for combing at destination. Each lot is tested by an independent laboratory before it is invoiced.

Mean fibre diameter is the first number every buyer asks for. The mill's usual production range covers the three bands below; individual lots are offered with their measured values.

Woollen and carded yarns, felt, blankets, upholstery and technical applications where bulk and resilience matter.

Finer or coarser lots may be available on request. Length, vegetable matter and colour are specified alongside fineness; see the certificate data below for the parameters reported per lot.

Processing at origin

From greasy wool to export bale.

Photographed at our partner mill in July 2026. The whole chain — sorting, scouring, carding, combing and pressing — happens under one roof in Uruguay.

  1. 01Sorting

    Fleeces are classed by fineness, length and contamination before they enter the line.

  2. 02Scouring

    Grease, suint and dirt are removed in a series of scour bowls; the wool is then dried.

  3. 03Carding & gilling

    Fibres are opened, aligned and drafted into a continuous sliver.

  4. 04Combing

    Short fibres and remaining vegetable matter are combed out; the result is the top.

  5. 05Press-packing

    Tops or scoured wool are press-packed into bales of approx. 400 kg, numbered and stored for loading.
